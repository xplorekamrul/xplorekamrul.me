      School Management Web App: everything a physical school runs on, moved into one platform.

      My role
      Full-stack, the full development cycle
      Live at
      birbd.org
      Hosting
      Self-hosted on Coolify

      What is inside

      Teaching

      Classes, subjects, schedules and a virtual classroom.

      Assessment

      Exams, quizzes, progress reports and automatic class promotion.

      Communication

      Built-in messaging and calling, a chatbot and a text editor.

      Operations

      Attendance, payments, and separate accounts for students, parents, teachers and admins.

      How it fits together

      The app itself: the pages, the server logic and the REST API, in one codebase.
      The school's data: classes, schedules, exams, attendance and payments.
      Sign-in, and the four kinds of account: student, parent, teacher and admin.
      Real-time messaging between accounts.
      Live audio and video for the virtual classroom and for calls.
      Email notifications.
      Self-hosting for both the app and its database.

      The hard parts

      • Building messaging, the text editor, quiz management and the chatbot in-house.
      • Integrating payments.
      • Shaping an architecture that holds all of it together.
      • Self-hosting the app and the database on Coolify.
